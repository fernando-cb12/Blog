Blog Entry 3: Week 3

This week was very short but it was interesting, I won't talk about the work in my project because it was mostly planning and refactoring some minnor stuff, but I will talk about the events that I attended this week.

The first event of the week was a masterclass about the Retail Global Industries Unit, here we learned about how the retail department is structured and operates. I learned about the different products, teams and how we work together to provide solutions and improve our existing products, beside of that, I learned about different technologies that we use in our products and how we use them to provide better solutions for our customers, also, I noticed where our products are used and how they are used in the real world everyday.

The second event of the week was a social networking event, where we had the opportunity to meet and talk with different people from different departments and teams, this was a very interesting event because I learned about different products and technologies that I didn't know about, also, I learned about different career paths and how people got to where they are today, my favorite part from this event is that it was in a go-karts track, so I had the opportunity to race for the first time and was a very fun experience, saddly I lost in the final race but I had a lot of fun and got closer to my colleagues.
