Blog Entry 4: First Merge

This week I had the opportunity to integrate the tool that I have been working on with the rest of the team. This means that it passed QA, so we are on the right path and have to keep working on it.

After that, we had a meeting with the team to discuss the next steps and what they expect from what we call phase 1.

Besides the work from this week, I had the opportunity to attend an event from a scholarship named MEXXICO Siglo XXI. We had multiple conferences and talks about different topics, with celebrities and important people from different areas. My favorite part was a talk from football players Guillermo Ochoa, Javier Aguirre, and Rafael Marquez. I identified with some of their stories and experiences in a certain way.
