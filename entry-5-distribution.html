Blog Entry 5: Distribution

This week, I had the opportunity to work on distributing the tool I have been developing. Since it is a test tool intended only for development environments, I had to consider how to make the setup as simple as possible. My first thought was to create a container image and share it with the team, but I realized that this would make the setup more complex for teammates who do not have a container engine.

When I was planning the project months ago, I considered sending builds and packages of the app, but that approach would not work without a proper local server. After reviewing my options with Codex and discussing them with senior engineers, I decided to research a way to distribute everything through a single executable without making the setup more complex.

I spent some time researching and testing different approaches until I found a library in the main framework I am using that could handle this. I implemented and tested it, and it worked as expected in less time than we had anticipated. I shared the result with my mentor, who was very happy with it, and my manager will be able to review it when he returns from vacation.
