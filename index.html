Onboarding

In this first entry, I'll talk about my first month rather than just this week, since things have been a bit calm so far. I also want to use this opportunity to introduce what this internship is about.

I started my internship in July, where I had the opportunity to meet people from all around the country, and even some from other countries, which was really nice. During my first day, I also met my recruiter and mentor.

The next day I received my laptop and started the onboarding process, which was a bit overwhelming at first, but I got through it quickly. Soon after, I was already meeting with my team and getting to know how we would be working together remotely.

By the end of my first week, I had completed all the required onboarding courses, and at the beginning of the second week, I had the application running locally with the help of my mentor. Once everything was set up, I continued exploring the project and met with my manager to discuss the work I would be contributing to. The project involves improving and modernizing an internal development tool used by my team.

My team is EFTLink (Electronic Funds Transfer), and I will link the public documentation because I want to make sure I only share information that is publicly available.

During the following weeks, I worked on a proof of concept to explore a new approach for the project. After receiving positive feedback from my team, I continued developing the idea and contributing to the main project. Since then, I have been working on designing, refactoring, and improving different aspects of the application.

This week, we are discussing the next steps for the project and how we can continue improving it. I'm looking forward to seeing how it evolves!
